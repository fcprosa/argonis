## Usage notes

- Replace `[COMPANY_SPECIFIC_HOOK]` with one sentence referencing a recent public event: licence grant, funding round, regulatory action, or hiring signal (LinkedIn job post for a Senior AML Analyst is a reliable pain indicator).
- Keep the rest of the message verbatim — personalisation in line 1 is enough.
- Send connection request first with no note; send the message only after acceptance.
- Follow-up once after 5 business days, one sentence: *"Sent this last week — still think it's relevant given [one-line reason]. Happy to drop a loom if easier."*
